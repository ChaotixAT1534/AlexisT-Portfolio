Pictogram

This was an assignment to create a pictogram from a headline on any article. I chose a The New York Times headline.

Get a Dog, Live Longer

I plan on doing this pictogram again because I feel like the message was not clear. When I was reading the article it said that older people have lower blood pressure when they own a dog. I really like how the emoji one turned out but I feel like there is a lot going on. Later, I tried making it simpler but just making outline of a person and a dog.
