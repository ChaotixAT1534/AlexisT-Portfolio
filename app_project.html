Welcome to HuntStress

I made an app aimed towards college students by identifying key struggles to manage such as transportation, finance, social connection, health, and academics.

What the App offers?

Finance: A section to track tuition payments, financial updates, budgeting resources, and campus job listings.

Transportation: Coming late to class? No Problem. This section provides nearby transportation options and estimates the quickest option available.

Social Media: I want students to have easy access to friends, professors, and advisors. This will be an easy space for them to make appointments or chat.

Health: Wellness resources, appointment scheduling, and access to campus health service

Academics: Class Schedules, assignment tracking, grades and academic resources

User Profile: Customize your profile with your own style. Can manage information, preferences, and notifications.
